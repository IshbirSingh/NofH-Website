Our Mission

Music as a gateway to happiness

At Notes of Happiness, our mission is to spread joy through music that reaches the ears of countless lives — whether that be young children, veterans, or our elderly. One note at a time, we aim to make music the gateway to happiness our community deserves.

Who We Are

Founded by two sisters

Founded by sisters Gunayan Kaur and Mayal Kaur, NofH is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Phoenix, AZ. Our EIN is 33-1979482. We believe that through NofH, music can spark smiles, uplift communities, and shape a better tomorrow — because music is the very color of our world.
